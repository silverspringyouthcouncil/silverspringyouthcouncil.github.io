Silver Spring Youth Council

Young people working together to shape the community we live in.

The Silver Spring Youth Council is a group of students from across Silver Spring, Maryland. We give young people a place to speak up, work on real community projects, and bring youth perspectives to the people who make local decisions.

[Your mission statement. Example: To empower Silver Spring youth to lead, serve, and advocate for a stronger, more inclusive community.]

Vision

[Your vision. Example: A Silver Spring where every young person is heard.]

Aims

Aim 1

Give every young person in Silver Spring a real voice in decisions that affect them.

Aim 2

Bring students from different schools and neighborhoods together.

Aim 3

Raise local issues that matter to youth and propose practical solutions.

Aim 4

Organize service projects that make a visible difference in our community.

Aim 5

Build leadership, public-speaking, and organizing skills in our members.

Aim 6

Educate community members about the principles of Sociocracy and give them an avenue where it can be practiced.

Aim 7

Connect young people with local leaders, officials, and organizations.

Aim 8

Help students learn how local government works and how to take part.

Aim 9

Equip youth with the skills to succeed and prosper in college and their careers after.

Aim 10

Work as a team, share responsibility, and follow through on our plans.
